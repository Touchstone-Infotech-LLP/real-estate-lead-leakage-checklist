# Real Estate Lead Leakage Scorecard

Count the number of questions where you answered **NO**.

## 0–7 NOs

### Funnel Under Control

You have a strong lead-management foundation.

Your next opportunity is improving campaign efficiency, conversion rates and deeper attribution.

---

## 8–14 NOs

### Revenue Leakage Likely

Your marketing may be generating demand, but gaps between marketing, pre-sales and sales are likely losing opportunities.

Fix the sections with the most NOs first.

---

## 15–21 NOs

### Significant Funnel Leakage

There are meaningful gaps in:

- Attribution
- Lead handling
- Qualification
- Follow-up
- Site visit management

Increasing ad spend before fixing these may simply generate more leakage.

---

## 22+ NOs

### Fix the Funnel Before Scaling

Your business may be relying heavily on:

- Individual salespeople
- Manual reporting
- Disconnected systems
- Unstructured follow-up

Establish visibility and accountability before aggressively scaling Meta or Google Ads.

---

## 5-Minute Reality Test

Pick one booking or completed site visit from the last 30 days.

Trace it backwards:

**Booking → Negotiation / Revisit → Completed Site Visit → Qualification → Sales Follow-Up → First Contact → Original Enquiry → Lead Source → Campaign / Ad**

If you can trace the full journey in a few minutes, your tracking infrastructure is in good shape.

If the trail disappears somewhere, that is one of the first places to investigate.
