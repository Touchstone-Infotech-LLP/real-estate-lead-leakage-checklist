# Real Estate Marketing Metrics

Real estate businesses should not evaluate marketing performance using cost per lead alone.

Track these four metrics instead.

## 1. Cost Per Lead

**Marketing Spend ÷ Total Leads**

---

## 2. Cost Per Qualified Lead

**Marketing Spend ÷ Qualified Leads**

---

## 3. Cost Per Completed Site Visit

**Marketing Spend ÷ Completed Site Visits**

---

## 4. Cost Per Booking

**Marketing Spend ÷ Bookings**

---

## Why These Metrics Matter

Lead volume alone does not tell you whether marketing is generating real business opportunities.

A campaign with a higher cost per lead may still be more valuable if it generates:

- More qualified buyers
- More site visits
- More negotiations
- More bookings

The objective is to connect advertising spend with actual sales outcomes.
