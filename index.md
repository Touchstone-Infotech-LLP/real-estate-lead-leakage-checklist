# Real Estate Lead Leakage Checklist

### Are your marketing leads actually turning into site visits and bookings?

A practical **35-point self-assessment for real estate agencies, channel partners and property marketing teams** to identify where leads are being lost between marketing and sales.

**Total Leads → Contactable → Relevant → Qualified → Site Visit → Booking**

---

## Why Use This Checklist?

Generating more leads does not necessarily generate more revenue.

If enquiries are not contacted quickly, qualified correctly, matched with suitable projects and followed up consistently, increasing your advertising budget may simply increase lead leakage.

This checklist helps you identify those gaps before you scale your marketing spend.

---

## What You'll Audit

The assessment covers seven areas of the real estate lead-to-revenue journey:

### 1. Marketing Attribution
Can you identify which source, campaign, ad or keyword generated each lead?

### 2. Lead Quality
Can you distinguish total leads from contactable, relevant and genuinely qualified buyers?

### 3. First Response & Sales Accountability
Do you know who received each lead, when they contacted it and what happened next?

### 4. Lead Qualification & Project Matching
Are prospects being qualified by budget, requirement and buying timeline — and matched with the right project?

### 5. Follow-Up & Lead Recovery
Are qualified prospects systematically followed up instead of disappearing from the pipeline?

### 6. Site Visit Management
Can you track scheduled, confirmed, completed, cancelled and rescheduled site visits?

### 7. Management & Revenue Visibility
Can management connect marketing sources with qualified leads, site visits and bookings?

## Measure More Than Cost Per Lead

Real estate marketing shouldn't be evaluated using CPL alone.

Track:

**Cost Per Lead**

Marketing Spend ÷ Total Leads

**Cost Per Qualified Lead**

Marketing Spend ÷ Qualified Leads

**Cost Per Completed Site Visit**

Marketing Spend ÷ Completed Site Visits

**Cost Per Booking**

Marketing Spend ÷ Bookings

## The 5-Minute Reality Test

Pick one booking or completed site visit from the last 30 days.

Try tracing it backwards:

**Booking → Negotiation / Revisit → Completed Site Visit → Qualification → Sales Follow-Up → First Contact → Original Enquiry → Lead Source → Campaign / Ad**

If you can trace the entire journey in a few minutes, your tracking infrastructure is probably in good shape.

If the trail disappears somewhere, you've identified one of the first areas worth investigating.
