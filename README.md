# Real Estate Lead Leakage Checklist

A practical 35-point self-assessment for real estate agencies, channel partners and property marketing teams.

The purpose of this checklist is to help identify where leads are being lost between:

**Lead Generation → Contact → Qualification → Site Visit → Booking**

Many real estate businesses focus heavily on cost per lead.

But a low cost per lead does not necessarily mean successful marketing.

If leads are not contacted quickly, qualified properly, matched with the right project and followed up consistently, increasing your advertising budget may simply increase the leakage.

## What This Checklist Covers

The assessment covers seven key areas:

1. Marketing Attribution
2. Lead Quality
3. First Response & Sales Accountability
4. Lead Qualification & Project Matching
5. Follow-Up & Lead Recovery
6. Site Visit Management
7. Management & Revenue Visibility

## Who Is This For?

This checklist is designed for:

- Real estate agencies
- Channel partners
- Property consultants
- Developer sales teams
- Pre-sales teams
- Real estate marketing teams
- Real estate business owners

## Key Funnel

The checklist evaluates your ability to track:

**Total Leads → Contactable Leads → Relevant Leads → Qualified Leads → Site Visits → Bookings**
