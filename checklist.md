# Real Estate Lead Leakage Checklist

Use this checklist to assess your journey from:

**Total Leads → Contactable → Relevant → Qualified → Site Visit → Booking**

Tick YES only when the process is consistently followed and you can verify it with data.

If something happens only occasionally, or depends on an individual salesperson remembering to do it, count it as a NO.

---

## 1. Marketing Attribution

### Do you know where your leads actually come from?

- [ ] Every lead automatically records its source: Meta, Google Ads, property portals, website, WhatsApp, referral or walk-in.
- [ ] For paid campaigns, we can identify the campaign that generated each lead.
- [ ] For Meta and Google leads, we can identify the specific ad, creative, keyword or campaign responsible where applicable.
- [ ] Our sales team can see the lead's marketing source before or while contacting the prospect.
- [ ] We can compare campaigns based on qualified leads and site visits rather than relying only on cost per lead.

### Ask Yourself

Which campaign generated the highest number of completed site visits last month?

If answering this requires manually matching multiple reports, you have an attribution gap.

---

## 2. Lead Quality

### Do you know the real quality of the leads you are paying for?

- [ ] We distinguish between total leads and contactable leads.
- [ ] We consistently record junk and invalid leads.
- [ ] We identify leads whose budget, property type or requirement does not match what we are selling.
- [ ] We distinguish genuine buyers from brokers, rental enquiries and other non-target leads.
- [ ] Our marketing reports show the full funnel beyond cost per lead.

### Why This Matters

Lead cost without lead quality can be misleading.

A campaign with a higher cost per lead may still be more profitable if it produces more qualified buyers and completed site visits.

---

## 3. First Response & Sales Accountability

### What happens in the first few minutes after an enquiry arrives?

- [ ] Every new lead is automatically assigned to a salesperson or pre-sales executive.
- [ ] We measure the time between lead generation and the team's first contact attempt.
- [ ] We have a defined first-call SLA for new enquiries.
- [ ] The number of call attempts and outcome of each attempt are recorded.
- [ ] Leads that remain unanswered or unattended are automatically flagged.
- [ ] If a salesperson does not act within the agreed timeframe, the lead can be escalated or reassigned.
- [ ] When pre-sales qualifies a lead and hands it to a salesperson, the handoff and ownership are clearly recorded.

### Quick Test

Pick 10 leads generated yesterday.

Can you quickly see:

**Who received it → When they first called → Number of attempts → Call outcome → Current status**

---

## 4. Lead Qualification & Project Matching

### Are you qualifying buyers, or simply collecting enquiries?

- [ ] We consistently capture the prospect's approximate budget.
- [ ] We capture property requirement and buying timeline.
- [ ] We know whether a prospect is a serious, qualified buyer before moving them further into the pipeline.
- [ ] A lead that does not fit the advertised project is checked against other suitable projects before being marked lost.
- [ ] Our team can identify alternative projects based on budget, location and requirement.

### Key Principle

The goal is not just lead-to-project tracking.

It is:

**Lead-to-right-project matching.**

---

## 5. Follow-Up & Lead Recovery

### What happens when a qualified prospect does not convert immediately?

- [ ] Every active lead has a clearly defined next follow-up date or action.
- [ ] Salespeople can immediately see which follow-ups are due or overdue.
- [ ] Prospects who do not answer the first few calls enter a structured follow-up process.
- [ ] WhatsApp, calls and other communication are used systematically during follow-up.
- [ ] Older qualified leads can be reactivated when a relevant project, offer or price change becomes available.

### Ask Your Team

How many qualified leads from the last 90 days currently have no next follow-up scheduled?

Those leads may represent pipeline leakage.

---

## 6. Site Visit Management

### Can you measure the journey from qualified lead to actual site visit?

- [ ] Site visits are tracked through clear stages.
- [ ] Salespeople or managers can see upcoming site visits and their current status.
- [ ] No-shows and cancelled visits have a defined follow-up or rescheduling process.
- [ ] Completed site visits have a recorded outcome and next action.
- [ ] Every completed site visit can be traced back to its original marketing source or campaign.

### The Key Marketing Question

Instead of asking only:

**What was our cost per lead?**

Ask:

**What did it cost us to generate a completed site visit?**

---

## 7. Management & Revenue Visibility

### Can management see performance without chasing the team for updates?

- [ ] Management can see leads by source, salesperson and pipeline stage from one reporting system.
- [ ] We can see conversion rates across lead, qualified lead, site visit and booking.
- [ ] We can identify which campaigns contribute to qualified leads, site visits and bookings rather than only form submissions.
